Sundays at TGP

Every Sunday is different. That's the point.

Most churches do the same service every week. We follow a monthly rhythm — a shared meal, living-room gatherings, a live worship experience, and a sabbath rest. Here's how it works.

The Fifth Table

Multiple locations · your table

What to expect: a simple guide sent that week · slow down · sacred space at home.

What It's Like to Show Up

No stage lights, no pressure, no dress code. You'll be greeted, you'll find a seat (probably at a table), and you can participate as much or as little as you want. Most gatherings run about 90 minutes.

  • Parking — free street parking near Proximity Collective.
  • Kids — kids are always welcome. We don't have separate kids programming right now, so little ones stay with you.
  • Will anyone single me out? — Never. Come observe, stay for the food.

Questions people actually ask

Sunday FAQ

How do I know where this week's gathering is?
Check this page — the "This month at a glance" list always shows the next gatherings, dates, and locations. Or join the text community and we'll tell you every week.
What should I wear?
Whatever you're comfortable in. Most people are casual — jeans are the norm, not the exception.
Is there anything for my kids?
Kids are always welcome at every gathering — First Fellowship especially is built for families. We don't currently have separate kids programming, so little ones stay with you.
What if I can't make it in person?
TGP LIVE streams every 3rd Sunday on YouTube and Facebook, and Petition prayer calls happen online every Monday. You can belong from anywhere.
How long do gatherings last?
Plan for about 90 minutes. First Fellowship can run longer — good meals do that. [Confirm timing.]
Do I have to sign up before coming?
No — just show up. But if you fill out the Plan Your Visit form, someone will be looking for you at the door, which makes walking in a lot easier.

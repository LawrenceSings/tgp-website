The Vision

Set Apart. A Reset.

Threshold is built on an old practice the Bible calls consecration. It simply means being set apart. Think of it as a reset. For four weeks we clear space, quiet the noise, and give God our full attention.

A door swings both ways. God opens the way, and we walk back through it. That is what RETURN is about: God knocks, we close what pulls us away, God turns our valleys into doors. Then we walk through, and we bring somebody with us.

And Joshua said to the people, "Sanctify yourselves, for tomorrow the LORD will do wonders among you."

Joshua 3:5 · NKJV

"I know your works. See, I have set before you an open door, and no one can shut it; for you have a little strength, have kept My word, and have not denied My name."

Fasting Guide

Choose Your Fast

Every Wednesday in Threshold is a fast day, and the fast breaks together at the evening gathering. Fasting doesn't earn God's attention. It's how we tell our appetites, "You don't run things. I'm returning."

"Now, therefore," says the LORD, "Turn to Me with all your heart, with fasting, with weeping, and with mourning." So rend your heart, and not your garments; return to the LORD your God, for He is gracious and merciful, slow to anger, and of great kindness. Joel 2:12-13 · NKJV

One Meal

Skip breakfast or lunch and spend that time in prayer.

Daytime Fast

No food from sunrise until we eat together at the gathering. Water throughout.

Daniel-Style Fast

Fruits, vegetables, grains, and water only for the day.

Non-Food Fast

Social media, streaming, or another daily habit. Give that time to God.

A word on health: anyone with diabetes or another medical condition, anyone pregnant or nursing, anyone on medication, and anyone with a history of disordered eating should choose a non-food fast or talk to a doctor first. A non-food fast is a full fast. No one is second class for choosing one.

Breaking the fast: start with lighter food at the gathering. Soup, bread, rice, fruit. Save the heavy dishes for later in the meal.

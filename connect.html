Grow Homes

Living Rooms, Deep Roots

On the 2nd and 4th weekends, TGP meets in living rooms across Minneapolis and Saint Paul — intimate worship, meaningful discussion, supportive community. This is where church gets personal.

Locations are shared when you sign up — neighborhood first, address after. Groups typically host 8–15 people.

Petition · Every Monday

A Church That Prays Together

Petition is our weekly Monday prayer call — the community gathers online to seek God, lift requests, and stand in faith with one another. Listen quietly or pray out loud; carrying something heavy or just wanting company — you're invited.
